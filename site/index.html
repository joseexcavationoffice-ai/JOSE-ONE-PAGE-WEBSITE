    FAQ

    Excavation & foundation questions, answered

    Still have a question about your site? Ask us directly. We'd rather answer it before the dig than after.

    Have more questions? Contact us (978) 401-8390
    Are you licensed and insured to work in Massachusetts?

    Yes. We hold all necessary licenses and comprehensive insurance to operate heavy machinery and perform structural foundation work across the state, ensuring total legal compliance.

    What is your service area?

    We're based in Milford and work on urban and suburban sites across Massachusetts, traveling up to a 50-mile radius. That covers the Milford area, MetroWest, Worcester County and the Blackstone Valley, and Greater Boston.

    Is the site evaluation free?

    Yes. The site evaluation is free. Send us the plans or a quick description, we talk through the scope by phone and then walk the site before giving you a timeline and budget.

    Do you work as a subcontractor for builders and general contractors?

    Yes. Builders and GCs bring us in for the excavation and foundation phase, from the first cut to foundation walls ready for framing. Send us the plans and your schedule and we'll fit the dig to it.

    What affects the cost of a basement excavation in Massachusetts?

    Mostly the depth and footprint of the dig, the soil and any rock on the lot, how much material has to be hauled away, groundwater, access for equipment on tight lots, and the schedule. We price each job after reviewing the plans and walking the site, so the number reflects your lot, not an average.

    Do you handle flatwork or concrete slabs?

    No. We specialize strictly in the heavy structural components: site excavation, deep basement construction, footings, foundation walls, and rebar reinforcement.

    Can you manage multiple investor properties at once?

    Yes. We have the heavy equipment fleet and experienced personnel required to handle multiple excavation sites simultaneously, keeping investor timelines strictly on track.

    How deep do foundations have to be in Massachusetts?

    Footings have to sit below the frost line, which is 48 inches below finished grade in most of Massachusetts. Your engineer's plans and the local building department set the final depth, and we dig and form to those numbers.

    What should I have ready before I request an evaluation?

    The property address, your target start date and, if you have them, the engineered foundation plan or site plan. If plans are still in progress, tell us where they stand and we'll let you know what we need to price the job.
